Open strategies Open history Open jobs alias

Launch backtests in template mode or saved mode through POST /api/backtests. Browser requests are sent to /api/* with credentials, while server-side auto_preflight_enabled may return explicit 202 Accepted background_auto fallback metadata and persisted run_id for history/runs navigation. Persisted /backtests/runs/{run_id} pages render the stage, execution_profile_mode, real 0..100% progress bar, and approximate eta_seconds after the launch response returns. This page does not stream mid-request sync progress because the current transport remains request/response only. Launch results stay summary-only; full report and trades stay on the dedicated persisted variant page.

Primary history lives at /backtests/history. Compatibility routes stay available at /backtests/jobs.
