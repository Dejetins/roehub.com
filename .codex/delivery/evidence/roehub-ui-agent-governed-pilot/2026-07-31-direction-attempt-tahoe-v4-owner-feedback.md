# Tahoe Backtests v4 owner feedback

- Date: `2026-07-31`.
- Checkpoint: `direction_selection`.
- Result: `visual_design_rejected_structure_preferred`.

The product owner identified `TAHOE-1 Workbench` as the strongest of the three information
architectures but rejected its overall visual design. The named failures were excessive vertical
stretch, a blue rather than black theme, insufficient adherence to current Linear styling, and
small semi-square radii that made the interface feel dated.

The replacement direction retains only the Jobs → Variants → Analysis product hierarchy and the
previously accepted functional inventory. It prohibits the v4 palette, dimensions, panel anatomy,
borders, row geometry, and radii. Current visual authority is
`docs/architecture/ui/roehub-linear-black-backtests-workbench-doctrine-v5.md` plus the named
product-owner and official Linear 2026 references.
