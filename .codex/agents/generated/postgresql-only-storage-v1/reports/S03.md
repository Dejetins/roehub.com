| External undocumented direct consumers | `unknown`; no external compatibility or CH merge parity inferred |

One transaction writes typed raw, deterministically deduplicates canonical keys,
updates actual/absence masks and exact summary deltas, and advances generation-bound
content revision only on materialized values/membership changes. Identical and newer
metadata-only replays do not perpetually invalidate artifacts. Funding revision is
separate and excludes provider-only fields not used by canonical funding arrays.
Generation lock ordering fences reset/rebuild; invalidated indexes cannot be declared
ready by an incremental write. Lost hints cannot erase the committed revision, but
revision-aware observation/reconciliation consumers are S04/S05, not already switched.
