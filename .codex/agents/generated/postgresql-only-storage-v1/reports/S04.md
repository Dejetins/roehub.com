| Unknown direct/external consumers | `unknown`: no unsupported compatibility inference |

Actual and source-absent masks remain distinct. Bounds/counts for artifacts use only
actual data. Partial days, exclusive in-minute cutoff and epoch zero use integer
minute arithmetic. Frontier advances only through a contiguous prefix, bounded to
seven days per confirmation; disjoint coverage cannot jump a gap. Confirmation
requires a live generation/token and callback before/after work. New empty symbols
bootstrap ready metadata; existing unknown/rebuilding indexes do not. Failed/lost
provider leases roll back; late actual data displaces absence. Rebuild invalidates
first, persists checkpoints and admits writes only after completion.

Source observation pages are limited to50 and carry durable generation/content/
funding revisions plus exact clipped bounds/count. Corrections of old values are
detected despite unchanged first/last/count and lost hints. Empty/unknown state
clears desired/published sources. Locked current-state checks and observed_at guards
reject stale delayed updates/tombstones. Fencing already-running artifact builds is
an S05 wiring obligation, not established by source-state clearing alone.

read including complete value assertions:18.171s, RSS increase0.08MiB. Transport
uses4096-row cursor batches or binary COPY and anonymous temporary disk; no
multi-year fetchall/duplicate tuple-array materialization. These are representative
observations, not percentiles, cold-cache guarantees or universal speedups. RSS is
sampled every50ms via ps and includes the harness/assertions; small peaks may be
missed. Result hashes identify serialized validation outputs (array repr may be
truncated); parity is established by complete assertions, not those hashes alone.

Large reads scale with output and use bounded timestamp partition scans. Ordered
tail seeks use summary bounds and LIMIT. Coverage/observation queries touch compact
masks/summaries instead of canonical history. Gap search expands731 days across
two years, never millions of minute rows. Explicit verifier/rebuild work is capped
